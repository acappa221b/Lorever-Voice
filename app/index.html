Lorever Voice Studio

How it works

1. Pick a zone on the left.

2. Press Record (or the space bar) and read the text aloud, at your own pace.

3. Press Stop (or the space bar again). The next text is ready for you.

4. When you are done, press Export (.zip). Unzip it into World of Warcraft\Interface\AddOns.

Silence at the start and the end is cut for you. You can record a text again at any time.
